MANUAL VERIFICATION QUICK GUIDE
===============================

Open Accuracy & OCR > Manual verification.

Needs review
  Shows only records with actionable OCR warnings. Source placeholders are not
  added to this queue unless the same record also has a real OCR warning.

Save & verify
  Saves the values currently in the form, records the changed fields and note,
  and refreshes both search indexes.

Verify as shown
  Keeps the stored field values and records that a person checked the source
  row. Any unsaved typing in the form is ignored by this button.

Verified
  Use the Status filter to review completed work and see its history.

Roll back
  Open Correction history and roll back the latest active correction. Older
  corrections become available for rollback one at a time, preserving a clear
  chronological audit trail.

Safety rules
  - The PDF itself is read-only and is never modified.
  - Duplicate serial numbers in the same PDF are rejected.
  - Ages outside 18 to 120 are rejected when an age is entered.
  - An old browser view cannot overwrite a newer edit.
  - A save is accepted only after SQLite and both Chroma collections update.
  - Automatic OCR improvement cannot replace a human-verified page.
